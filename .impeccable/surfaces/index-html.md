Mode: experience. Visitor: recruiter or hiring manager skimming candidates. Action: email Abhishek about a full-time AI engineering role. Proof: measured results from data/systems.json, drawn as counted pictograms. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, content from data/systems.json, short scroll, ask-AI box removed for now, case-study copy is provisional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pictorial statistics. Every result is counted out in rows of identical pictograms (one symbol = one fixed unit), so a recruiter understands the work in one glance without reading. Refuses the dev-portfolio default of name hero + project card grid + paragraphs.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink, flat Isotype blue for "before / baseline", Isotype red for "after / result / action", ochre and green as further categories. Flat filled pictogram silhouettes, no outlines, no gradients, no shadows, no rounded cards. Geometric sans (Jost, Futura lineage) in caps for labels and headings. Charts sit on plain ground with a key line ("each clock = 10 minutes").

STORY: Understands in seconds that he gives teams their afternoons back with AI systems that run in production; believes it because the numbers are counted, labelled, and honest (indexed figures say so); clicks "Hire me" (mailto) which is visible in header, hero and close.

Isotype chart at full width: BY HAND row of 15 blue clocks, AUTOMATED row of 1.5 red clocks, key line. Bottom: 50+ tickets/hour and 87% figures left, black "Hire me" block right.
